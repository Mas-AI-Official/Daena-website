### Background Music Recommendations:
- Subtle ambient/jazz tracks at 10-15% volume
- No distracting beats or vocals
- Professional, sophisticated tone
- Fade in/out smoothly with narration

- Pause 2-3 seconds between major points
- Allow time for visual elements to load
- Match narration pace with on-screen actions
- Include brief silence for emphasis on key points
