## Implementation Notes

### **Voice Style Guidelines:**
- **Tone**: Confident, data-driven, investor-focused
- **Pacing**: Slightly slower for technical content, faster for benefits
- **Emphasis**: Highlight key numbers, percentages, and competitive advantages
- **Pauses**: Allow 2-3 seconds after key statistics for impact

### **Visual Cues:**
- **Cost Analysis**: Show charts, graphs, and comparison tables
- **Security**: Display certifications, encryption symbols, compliance badges
- **Market**: Use market size graphics, growth curves, and validation metrics
- **Funding**: Show use of funds pie chart, revenue projections, and ROI calculations

1. **Unique Technology**: SFHC architecture vs. traditional systems

3. **Enterprise Security**: SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA compliance
4. **Market Validation**: $165B SAM with achievable 2% target
