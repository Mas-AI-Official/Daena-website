Building the Future of AI-Native Business Operations

Mas-AI Technology Inc. is developing Daena and VibeAgent—revolutionary AI systems that enable any business to operate with an intelligent AI Vice President that learns, governs, and scales.

Vision

AI-Native VP for Every Founder

We envision a world where every business—from startups to enterprises—can operate with an intelligent AI Vice President that handles strategic decisions, coordinates teams of specialized AI agents, and learns from experience while maintaining governance and auditability.

Daena and VibeAgent make this vision a reality by combining:

  • Unique Sunflower-Honeycomb architecture (patent-pending)
  • Multi-LLM brain with consensus mechanisms
  • Built-in governance, safety, and audit systems
  • Visual no-code interface for non-technical users

Technology

Daena: AI-Native VP

  • Sunflower-Honeycomb Architecture: 8 departments × 6 agents = 48 specialized AI agents with hexagonal communication
  • Multi-LLM Brain: Intelligent routing across 10+ LLM models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, local models) with consensus mechanisms
  • Governance & Safety: Proactive and reactive governance with council escalation, legal gates, and audit trails
  • Memory System (NBMF): 3-tier memory (hot/warm/cold) with 99.5%+ accuracy and 60%+ cost savings

VibeAgent: Visual Agent Builder

  • Visual No-Code Builder: Drag-and-drop interface for creating AI agents and workflows
  • Intelligent Compilation: Plain-English "vibe" input → visual agent blueprint
  • Legal/Ethics Gates: Automatic risk assessment, consent management, compliance
  • Real-time Monitoring: Live event streaming, pause/resume controls, analytics

Why Now

The AI Orchestration & Governance Gap: Current AI solutions are fragmented—single-LLM systems lack diversity, agent frameworks lack governance, and no-code tools lack intelligence. Businesses need integrated systems that combine intelligence, coordination, and governance.

Market Timing: The AI agent market is growing rapidly ($XX billion, XX% CAGR), but most solutions are either too technical (requiring coding) or too limited (lacking governance). Daena and VibeAgent address this gap with a unique combination of intelligence, governance, and accessibility.

Technology Readiness: Multi-LLM APIs are now mature, local models are becoming powerful, and governance frameworks are emerging. The time is right to build integrated AI-native business operating systems.
